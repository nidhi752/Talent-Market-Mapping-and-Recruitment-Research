# Talent Market Mapping & Recruitment Research

An Excel-based market research and recruitment analytics project focused on India's Enterprise AI, Generative AI, and SaaS ecosystem.

The project analyses 44 companies, maps seven job roles across 21 skills, and develops a structured framework for identifying potential recruitment clients and understanding talent demand.

## Key Features
- **Talent Market Mapping:** Organised company research by industry, location, and business category.
- **Job Role & Skills Analysis:** Compared seven professional roles across 21 skills to identify common requirements and specialised skill sets.
- **Client Prospect Prioritisation:** Built a weighted scoring model to rank potential recruitment clients using company size, role coverage, estimated agency openness, and hiring signals.
- **Recruitment Workflow Design:** Created a sample job order, candidate screening scorecard, and sourcing strategy.
- **Recruitment Funnel Analysis:** Modelled candidate progression from initial sourcing to client shortlisting.
- **Market Insights:** Developed a research report highlighting potential hiring opportunities, target markets, and areas requiring further validation.

## Tools & Skills
Microsoft Excel · Market Research · Data Analysis · Talent Mapping · Recruitment Research · Client Prospecting · Weighted Scoring Models · Business Intelligence

## Objective
To demonstrate how structured research and data-driven analysis can support recruitment planning, client prioritisation, and talent acquisition strategy.

**Note:** Company rankings, hiring signals, and recruitment funnel figures are research estimates or illustrative assumptions unless independently verified. The project is intended for research and analytical demonstration, not as a source of confirmed live vacancies.
